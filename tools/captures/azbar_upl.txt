Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL)
Regulation of Nonlawyers

Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL): No person may practice law in the State of Arizona or represent in any way that they may practice law in the State of Arizona unless that person is an active member of the state bar or is otherwise authorized to do so by the Rules of the Supreme Court of Arizona.

To check if an individual is authorized to practice law in the State of Arizona, click here.

For more information, contact the State Bar of Arizona by phone at 602.340.7280, or by email at lawyerinfo@staff.azbar.org.
